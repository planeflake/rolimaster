# The Blood King

The Blood King is an ancient blood sorcerer, tyrant, or revenant whose power depends on blood, royal inheritance, and the changing aspects of mana flowing from [[The Nine Fonts]].

He is not equally strong in every cycle. His empire is tidal.

## Core Threat

The Blood King uses dark blood magic to influence and control people through blood samples, inherited bonds, exposed wounds, oaths, and royal lineage.

His agents, the [[Red Wardens]], conceal his work behind civic discipline, plague law, public health, and hereditary record keeping.

## The Royal Bloodline Samples

The Blood King possesses samples from 7 of the 11 royal bloodlines. These samples are stored in [[The Red Reliquary]] beneath [[Veyrholm]].

If he gains all 11 samples, he may be able to:

- Command royal descendants across the world.
- Keep his blood magic stable across all mana cycles.
- Open the Red Reliquary without risk.
- Create a false, cycle-proof crown.
- Turn the Crimson Vow into a true magical priesthood.
- Force a city or kingdom into a permanent Red Return.

The missing four royal lines are therefore both his weakness and his obsession.

## The Thinning

When the mana cycle turns against him, the Red Wardens call it the Thinning.

During the Thinning:

- His detection weakens.
- Blood-seals grow brittle.
- Stored samples pale, clot, or crystallize.
- Red Wardens lose reliable access to higher workings.
- People remember thoughts and loyalties that had been suppressed.
- The Red Reliquary becomes more vulnerable, but less predictable.

This is when he takes risks.

## The Red Return

When the cycle favors him, the Red Wardens call it the Red Return.

During the Red Return:

- His detection sharpens.
- Old blood stains reappear.
- Royal bloodline samples grow warm and vivid.
- The Red Reliquary strengthens.
- Repeated unauthorized blood magic is quickly noticed.
- Marked people suffer dreams, headaches, and sudden obedience.

This is when his enemies hide.

## Mana Aspect Effects

| Dominant Aspect | Blood Magic Behavior | Blood King Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Essence | Physical, ritualistic, and forceful | Less subtle and more visibly bloody |
| Mentalism | Dreamlike, emotional, and intimate | Physical rites weaken |
| Channeling | Oath-bound, sacramental, and hierarchical | Depends more on belief, vows, and willing hierarchy |
| Arcane | Ancient, complete, and unstable | Powerful but risky; old safeguards may awaken |

## The Vault Seal

The Red Reliquary is sealed with the Blood King's own blood. His blood is required to open it cleanly.

Other possible openings are dangerous:

- A willing King's Warden.
- A false crown rite using all 11 royal bloodline samples.
- A catastrophic counter-ritual involving one of the Nine Fonts.

False blood may clot into black glass or cause the vault to seal intruders inside.

## Campaign Uses

- The Blood King sends Red Wardens into Aethergate during the Thinning to recover a missing bloodline sample.
- A royal descendant in [[The Smouldering Cog]] does not know what they are.
- Someone steals Warden blood and begins dreaming another person's life.
- A Veyrholm nursery rhyme appears in a place where no one should know it.
- The party learns that the best time to strike the Red Reliquary is also when its safeguards are least predictable.
