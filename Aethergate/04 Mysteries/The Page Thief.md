# The Page Thief

## Overview

The Page Thief is stealing the same numbered page from unrelated books across Aethergate.

At first the thefts seem petty: page seven from a cookbook, page seven from a canal map, page seven from a devotional pamphlet, page seven from a guild charter. The pattern becomes dangerous when the [[Living Ink Guild]] realizes the stolen pages form a composite ritual when laid in the order of their original publication dates.

## Pattern

- Only one page is removed from each book.
- The binding is never damaged.
- Witnesses remember hearing paper move, but no footsteps.
- Replacement pages are sometimes left behind, copied in a perfect but unfamiliar hand.
- Books made with [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] resist the theft unless the thief knows their true title.

## Possible Truths

- A living manuscript is gathering missing instructions for its own completion.
- Someone is building a ritual from fragments so no single theft looks dangerous.
- The stolen pages are not being taken by a person, but summoned by a command hidden in old ink.
- The thief is trying to remove references to a forgotten civic office from every surviving book.

## Campaign Uses

- A stolen page is found folded into a tiny walking figure near [[The Smouldering Cog]].
- The next target is a legal book inside the [[Living Ink Guild]] archive.
- The party must protect a book overnight while its missing page tries to call itself free.
- The missing pages include a clue to [[The Unburnable Ledger]].
