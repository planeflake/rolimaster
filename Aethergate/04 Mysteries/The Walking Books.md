# The Walking Books

## Overview

Books in the [[Living Ink Guild]] library are being enchanted into living manuscripts and literally walking out of the archive.

Most escapees are awkward things: ledgers waddling on bent covers, manuals crawling like hinged boxes, scrolls inching under doors, and slim chapbooks fluttering through vents. The comedy ends when the missing books begin choosing their own destinations.

## Signs

- Empty shelves with no broken locks.
- Ink footprints on desks, window ledges, and courier slots.
- Bookmarks tied into knots from the inside.
- Titles that change after the book has escaped.
- Fresh [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] folded into crude legs or wings.

## Possible Causes

- Someone is testing early [[Living Ink]] enchantments on guild books.
- A stolen batch of [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] was tainted at [[Moonmere Paper Mill]].
- The books are fleeing because they know [[The Page Thief|the Page Thief]] is coming.
- A living index inside the archive has begun reorganizing the library by destiny rather than subject.

## Campaign Uses

- The party is hired to recover escaped books without damaging them.
- A runaway manual leads the party to a hidden door in the guildhall.
- A living book begs not to be returned.
- One book walks directly to a person named in [[The Unburnable Ledger]] and refuses to leave.
