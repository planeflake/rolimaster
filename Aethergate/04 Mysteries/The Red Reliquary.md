# The Red Reliquary

The Red Reliquary is the hidden vault beneath [[Veyrholm]], publicly concealed behind civic archives, genealogical records, infirmary storage, and sealed Warden rooms.

Officially, it is only Archive Room Seven.

## Contents

The vault contains:

- Blood samples from 7 of the 11 royal bloodlines.
- Preserved blood from ranked [[Red Wardens]].
- Blood-written genealogies.
- Oath records from the Crimson Vow.
- Relics used to stabilize [[The Blood King]] during the Thinning.
- Failed samples that have clotted into black glass.

## Seal

The Red Reliquary is sealed with the Blood King's own blood. His blood is required to open the vault cleanly.

The seal may also answer to:

- A willing King's Warden.
- A false crown rite using all 11 royal bloodline samples.
- A catastrophic ritual involving one of [[The Nine Fonts]].

False blood may cause the vault to seal intruders inside, rewrite memories, or clot into black glass.

## Mana Cycle Behavior

During the Thinning:

- Lesser locks fail or become brittle.
- The Blood King's detection weakens.
- Stored samples pale, clot, or crystallize.
- Hidden compartments may open by accident.

During the Red Return:

- The Blood King's blood-seal strengthens.
- Stored samples grow warm and vivid.
- Blood constructs or old Warden oaths may awaken.
- Intruders may be marked before they know they have been seen.

## Campaign Uses

- A map to Archive Room Seven turns up in Aethergate with no mention of Veyrholm.
- A Red Warden asks the party to destroy one royal sample before the Red Return.
- A royal descendant's blood opens a lesser chamber but also wakes something deeper.
- Someone offers a vial that can open the Reliquary, but the vial may contain the Blood King's blood.
