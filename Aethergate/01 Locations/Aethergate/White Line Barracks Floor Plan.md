# White Line Barracks Floor Plan

![[Maps/White Line Barracks Floor Plan.png]]

## The Building

The interior plan of [[White Line Barracks]], home post of the [[White Line Wardens]]. The barracks sits between [[Aetherspark's Tower]] and the [[Ninefold Infirmary]], guarding the nearest Heartline valves and the hospital feed.

It is built to empty fast. Every corridor runs toward a gate, and the alarm bells can be heard from the surgery halls.

## Ground Floor

- **Muster Yard**: drill posts shaped like illegal siphon clamps, polearm racks, and the emergency canister carts kept ready to roll.
- **Watch Room**: the duty board, the Heartline valve map, and the resonance lanterns charged and waiting.
- **Holding Cells**: short-stay cells for siphon crews and sabotage suspects before transfer to the [[Mana Authority]].

## Second Floor

- **Warden Quarters**: bunks, kit lockers, and the white-enamel armour racks.
- **Commander's Office**: the Warden-Commander's room, where escort orders and hospital-priority calls are signed.
- **Mercy Room**: a quiet shrine-corner for Wardens who lost a valve, a crew, or a ward.

## Lower Level

- **Canister Vault**: emergency Essence canisters under heavy seal, keyed to hospital engineers.
- **Shear Armoury**: mana-shear polearms and seal-runes for cutting illegal clamps.
- **The Tunnel Gate**: a sealed Warden access into the [[The Underworks|Underworks]], opened only when the hospital feed is threatened.
