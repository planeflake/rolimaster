# The Meterhouse Floor Plan

![[Maps/The Meterhouse Floor Plan.png]]

## The Building

The Meterhouse is the seat of the [[Mana Authority]], a squat, fortified hall of grey stone and sealed brass standing in the shadow of [[Aetherspark's Tower]]. Every door is weighed, stamped, and logged. Citizens come here to be licensed, taxed, audited, or accused.

The air smells of ledger wax, lamp oil, and the faint ozone of confiscated canisters.

## Ground Floor

- **Licensing Hall**: queues, stamping desks, and the long counter where refiners and array operators renew their permits.
- **Weighing Room**: certified scales and resonance gauges for measuring declared canisters against their papers.
- **Public Records Counter**: where citizens petition for copies of quotas, fines, and inspection reports.

## Second Floor

- **Inspectors' Bullpen**: cluttered desks, case boards, and the duty roster for field inspections.
- **Director's Office**: a cold, well-ordered room where the Authority's hardest decisions are signed.
- **Interview Cells**: spare rooms for questioning smugglers, informants, and unlucky license-holders.

## Vaults

- **The Impound**: confiscated inhalers, siphon devices, and illegal canisters, each tagged as evidence.
- **Seal Archive**: master copies of every valid Authority seal, guarded against forgery.
- **Quarantine Bay**: a warded room for canisters that read wrong — overcharged, decayed, or simply impossible.
