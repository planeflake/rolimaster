# The Tallyhouse Floor Plan

![[Maps/The Tallyhouse Floor Plan.png]]

## The Building

The Tallyhouse is the working hall of the [[Ratio Office]], a tall, narrow building of shelves and silence. It is the quietest important place in [[Aethergate]]: the only sounds are pen scratch, abacus click, and the slow turn of the reserve drums.

Everyone who matters in the city has stood in its lobby waiting to learn whether their district will dim.

## Ground Floor

- **Petition Lobby**: where district stewards, factory factors, and shrine-keepers wait to plead their case.
- **Posting Wall**: the public board where rationing notices and conversion schedules are nailed up each cycle.
- **Intake Desks**: clerks log incoming reserve reports from every reservoir and array in the city.

## Second Floor

- **The Counting Floor**: rows of tally clerks and abacus frames; the city's mana is added and subtracted here all day.
- **Forecast Room**: charts of [[Mana Decay]], conversion timing, and shortage projection.
- **Chief Rationer's Office**: where the final ration figures are sealed and sent to the [[Mana Authority]].

## Lower Archive

- **The Reserve Drums**: great rotating ledgers recording the true reserve of every realm and grade, double-locked.
- **Sealed Forecast Vault**: confidential projections that, if leaked, would start a panic or a riot.
- **The Discrepancy Room**: a small office where the few numbers that refuse to balance are quietly investigated.
