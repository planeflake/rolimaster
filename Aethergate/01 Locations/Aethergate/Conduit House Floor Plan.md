# Conduit House Floor Plan

![[Maps/Conduit House Floor Plan.png]]

## The Building

Conduit House is the guildhall of the [[Guild of Conduits]], built directly over a junction of three old pressure mains in the Pipeline Quarter. The whole structure hums. Apprentices learn to sleep through it; visitors never do.

It is less a hall than a machine someone decided to live inside. Brass gauges line every corridor, and a master pressure board in the entry reads the health of half the district at a glance.

## Ground Floor

- **Gauge Hall**: the public face — a wall of district pressure dials, a duty desk, and a bench of citizens reporting leaks and damp walls.
- **Dispatch Cage**: where repair crews are assigned, tools signed out, and emergency calls rung up on the alarm bells.
- **Tool Vault**: mana-shears, clamp kits, sounding horns, and sealed patch-runes, all chained and inventoried.
- **The Wet Room**: a tiled bay where leaking salvage is brought in, drained, and made safe.

## Second Floor

- **Drafting Loft**: long tables of pipe schematics, many hand-corrected and contradicting the city's official maps.
- **Pressure Board Room**: the master board and the senior engineers who read it; the guild's real decisions happen here.
- **Apprentice Dormitory**: cramped bunks above the mains, warm and never quiet.

## Lower Works

- **Junction Floor**: the three pressure mains meet here behind glass-fronted valves; standing on the grating, you can feel the city breathe.
- **The Listening Cells**: small soundproofed rooms where leak-listeners train their ears against recorded pipe noise.
- **Old Map Stack**: rolled schematics from earlier Cycles, some showing conduits no modern engineer will admit exist.

## Sealed Sub-Level

- **The Dead Junction**: a capped mainline the guild stopped using generations ago. Lately its gauges have begun to twitch.
