# Ninefold Infirmary

## Overview

The Ninefold Infirmary is Aethergate's main hospital complex, fed by [[The Heartline]] and guarded closely by the [[White Line Wardens]].

Citizens often call it the Ninefold Hospice when speaking of its charity wards, hospice rooms, and long-care halls.

The Infirmary treats ordinary wounds, mana sickness, industrial accidents, failed spellwork, prosthetic injuries, and mysterious ailments caused by unstable Font cycles.

## Public Role

- Emergency surgery and triage
- Mana sickness treatment
- Charity care for workers and the poor
- Prosthetic tuning and repair
- Quarantine for unstable magical conditions
- Long-term care for patients harmed by infrastructure failures

## Atmosphere

The building is bright, crowded, and tense. White lamps burn over the surgery halls, colored curtains mark different treatment wards, and pressure bells are watched as carefully as patients.

The public halls smell of boiled linen, copper, bitter herbs, and clean Essence.

## Current Tension

Recent mana thefts and strange pressure reports have made the Infirmary nervous. The Heartline still holds, but some healers say the Essence feels thin, as if it has already passed through too many hands.

## Campaign Uses

- The party takes paid charity work when they need quick passage money.
- A healer asks for discreet help before reporting a Heartline fault.
- A patient describes dreams that match an Underworks route below Cogmarket.
- The Infirmary offers a favor or travel writ instead of coin.
