# Inkwell House Floor Map

![[Maps/Inkwell House Floor Map.png]]

## Overview

Inkwell House is the guildhall of the [[Living Ink Guild]]. It stands near Cogmarket, close enough to the noise of trade to serve merchants and couriers, but inward-facing enough to protect its archives.

## Ground Floor

- **Public Copy Hall**: long counters where citizens request copies, seals, notices, and certified readings.
- **Waiting Benches**: crowded with merchants, clerks, messengers, and worried heirs.
- **Wax Desk**: contracts are sealed, witnessed, and tagged for archive routing.
- **Courier Slot Wall**: small brass doors for paired pages, official slips, and messenger tubes.
- **Supply Window**: [[Bramm Vellumthumb]] controls paper, ink, wax, quills, nibs, and ledger chains from here.

## Second Floor

- **Scriptorium**: journeymen copy books and civic forms beneath north-facing lamps.
- **Illumination Room**: color work, diagrams, title pages, maps, and heraldic papers.
- **Apprentice Desks**: cramped, supervised, and always dusted with page sand.
- **Proofing Gallery**: senior scribes inspect copies before certification.

## Third Floor

- **Inkbinders' Hall**: warded writing desks for magical calligraphy and [[Living Ink]] practice.
- **Seal Chamber**: contract seals, oath marks, and tamper-proofing work.
- **Quiet Room**: a small warded room for documents that speak, whisper, or object.
- **Guildmistress's Office**: [[Ysabet Blackletter]] receives dangerous clients here.

## Basement Archive

- **Public Records Stack**: older but non-secret records available by petition.
- **Restricted Cabinets**: sealed contracts, dangerous copies, and disputed documents.
- **Ledger Vault**: chained ledgers and account books, watched by ink wards.
- **Repair Tables**: damaged books are flattened, mended, dried, and coaxed into order.

## Deep Stack

The deepest floor is older than the visible guildhall. Its stones may predate the current Cycle.

- **Cycle Vaults**: documents from earlier Cycles.
- **Sleeping Manuscript Room**: living books are kept closed, wrapped, and named.
- **Old Sluice Door**: a sealed drainage passage whose markings resemble those at [[Moonmere Paper Mill]].
- **Black Shelf**: items too legally important to destroy and too dangerous to display.
