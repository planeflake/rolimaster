# Veyrholm

Veyrholm is a prosperous riverside settlement of pale stone streets, excellent physicians, careful butchers, and immaculate civic habits.

Outsiders usually remember it as clean, safe, polite, and oddly quiet. Locals are taught from childhood that blood must not be spilled, shown, named casually, or left uncovered in public.

> Red is for roses, wine, and thread.  
> Never for hands, and never for dead.

## Public Face

Veyrholm presents itself as a model settlement of public health and civic discipline.

- White gloves are common among officials, physicians, butchers, and civic servants.
- Butchers work behind sealed red-lacquer doors.
- Surgeons advertise bloodless methods and private recovery rooms.
- Duels, public executions by blade, and open slaughter are forbidden.
- Hunters drain game outside the settlement boundary.
- Every home keeps white cloths and a porcelain washbasin near the door.

## The Blood Taboo

The taboo is not merely superstition.

Blood exposed to open air within Veyrholm can become magically legible to [[The Blood King]]. If he has a sample, oath, inherited claim, or other blood connection to the person, he can use the exposed blood as a point of influence.

Public bloodshed may allow him to:

- Heighten fear, loyalty, shame, or anger.
- Track the bleeding person.
- Strengthen old marks, vows, or curses.
- Send dreams through a blood connection.
- Stir ancestral memories.
- Notice unauthorized blood magic.

The taboo protects the settlement, but it also keeps the people frightened, compliant, and dependent on the institutions that secretly serve him.

## The White Ward

The oldest district of Veyrholm is called the White Ward. Its public institutions include an infirmary, a records house, a laundry court, and a civic archive.

Secretly, the White Ward houses [[The Red Reliquary]], the vault used by [[The Blood King]] and the [[Red Wardens]].

## The Red Reliquary

The Red Reliquary contains royal bloodline samples, Warden blood stores, oath records, and other relics of blood magic.

It currently holds blood samples from 7 of the 11 royal bloodlines. The missing four lines are a major weakness in the Blood King's plan.

The vault is sealed with the Blood King's own blood. His blood is the true key. Without it, the vault can only be opened by dangerous substitutes such as a willing King's Warden, a counterfeit rite using all 11 royal bloodline samples, or a catastrophic ritual involving one of [[The Nine Fonts]].

## Mana Cycle Effects

The Blood King's power waxes and wanes with the shifting mana aspects of the Nine Fonts. Veyrholm exists partly to preserve his influence when the cycle turns against him.

During the Thinning, when his blood magic weakens:

- Blood-seals require more blood to open.
- Stored samples pale, clot, or crystallize.
- Red Wardens lose reliable access to higher workings.
- Minor blood magic is harder for the Blood King to detect.
- Victims recover memories that had been suppressed.
- The vault door sweats clear water instead of blood.

During the Red Return, when his power strengthens:

- Old blood stains reappear through paint and stone.
- The Red Reliquary becomes harder to breach.
- Royal descendants suffer headaches, dreams, and sudden obedience.
- Stored samples grow warm and vivid.
- The Blood King can detect repeated unauthorized casting.

## Campaign Uses

- A visitor cuts themselves in the market and the entire square moves with practiced calm to hide the blood.
- A royal heir passes through Veyrholm and leaves strangely obedient.
- A midwife tries to smuggle a newborn away before its blood is registered.
- The party hears the children's rhyme in Aethergate before anyone admits where it comes from.
- The Blood King needs one of the four missing royal bloodline samples before the next Red Return.
