# Moonmere Paper Mill

## Overview

Moonmere Paper Mill is a reed-paper workshop outside [[Aethergate]], set beside a shallow silver marsh where moonlight pools in the water on clear nights.

The mill makes [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]], a prized writing material used by the [[Living Ink Guild]] for warded contracts, paired correspondence pages, living manuscripts, and delicate mixed-realm spell notation.

The road from Aethergate reaches Moonmere in less than a day by cart, but the last mile crosses raised plankways through whispering reeds and half-sunken stone markers from earlier Cycles.

## The Craft

Moonmere's papermakers harvest pale reeds at dusk, pulp them with clean water, press them into sheets, and treat them with a mordant made from moon-salt, powdered shell, and a little controlled mana residue.

The sheets are then laid in open frames beneath moonlight. Good paper drinks the light evenly. Bad paper curls, bruises, or shows tiny crawling letters before any ink touches it.

## Notable Features

### The Lying Reeds

The reeds around the mill sometimes repeat words spoken nearby, but not always accurately.

### The Silver Drying Frames

Finished sheets rest in tall outdoor frames during the mordanting nights. Damaging a frame can ruin an entire season's supply.

### The Old Sluice

A stone water-control gate from a previous Cycle still feeds the marsh. Nobody at the mill fully understands its markings.

### The Night House

The papermakers sleep in a long, low house with shutters painted white to reflect moonlight away from fresh sheets.

## People

- **Maer Tallowreed**: practical mill-master who trusts weather, paper, and silence more than city officials.
- **Nessa Whitepulp**: apprentice who can tell a sheet's quality by sound when it flexes.
- **Old Cadrin**: retired scribe from the [[Living Ink Guild]] who came to Moonmere for quiet and found none.

## Current Trouble

Moonmere's newest batch of paper will not stay still.

Fresh sheets twitch in their frames, fold themselves into crude legs, and try to walk north toward Aethergate before dawn. The papermakers blame bad mordant, but Old Cadrin thinks someone has written a command into the water feeding the mill.

## Campaign Uses

- The party must escort a wagon of [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] back to the [[Living Ink Guild]] before rain or thieves ruin it.
- The mill needs rare moon-salt from a half-drowned shrine in the marsh.
- Living sheets escape the drying frames and fold themselves into birds, masks, or tiny paper servants.
- The old sluice begins opening only when someone reads its markings aloud.
- A stolen page from the [[Unburnable Ledger]] is found soaking in the pulp vat, unharmed.
