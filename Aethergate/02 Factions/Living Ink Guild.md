# Living Ink Guild

## Overview

The Living Ink Guild is Aethergate's licensed fellowship of scribes, contract-calligraphers, copyists, illuminators, archivists, document wardens, and magical penhands.

Its members preserve records, certify contracts, copy civic plans, bind legal seals, restore damaged manuscripts, and prepare the fine written forms needed for mixed magic such as [[Living Ink]].

The guild's old saying is:

> Ink remembers what breath denies.

## Guildhall

The guildhall, called the Inkwell House, stands near the edge of Cogmarket where legal offices, map stalls, courier desks, and repair shops crowd against the older pipeline streets.

Its public floor smells of lampblack, gum, drying vellum, hot wax, and polished brass. The front hall is always busy with apprentices sanding pages, clerks checking names against fee ledgers, and journeymen copying notices for the city boards.

Below the public rooms are climate-stable archive vaults lined with warded cabinets. The deepest stacks are said to contain contracts signed during earlier Cycles, books that rewrite their own indexes, and pages that cannot be burned until every named debt upon them is settled.

## Public Role

- Copy and certify legal documents
- Maintain contract seals and public notices
- Restore damaged records, maps, and ledgers
- Train scribes, illuminators, and magical calligraphers
- Provide neutral witnesses for guild agreements
- Prepare warded pages, courier slips, and sealed letters
- Assist the [[Ratio Office]] with rationing notices and archival tables

## Magical Practice

The guild teaches that ink can hold mana because writing gives intention a body.

Most members are mundane scribes with excellent hands and sharp memories. A smaller circle, called the Inkbinders, learns to make script stir, conceal, copy, answer, or bind. They are careful to distinguish their craft from runes, which the guild considers heavier, older, and more dangerous.

Inkbinders often work at the boundary between Essence structure, Mentalism meaning, and Channeling authority. This makes them natural users of mixed written magic, especially the [[Living Ink]] spell list.

## Reputation

The Living Ink Guild is respected, useful, and quietly feared.

Merchants trust its seals. Advocates argue over its copies. Priests request its oath scripts. Smugglers hate its audit marks. The [[Mana Authority]] uses guild-certified forms whenever a ration, license, or inspection must survive public challenge.

Common folk call guild scribes "inkfingers" whether or not they belong to the guild.

## Known Services

- Clean a stained page
- Copy a letter, map, diagram, or spell notation
- Identify likely forgery
- Seal a contract against tampering
- Prepare paired correspondence pages
- Restore damaged family records
- Read old hands and forgotten civic abbreviations
- Hide a message in a harmless-looking margin

## Relationships

- Works closely with the [[Ratio Office]] on public notices and ration records.
- Certifies some paperwork for the [[Mana Authority]], but dislikes emergency orders written in haste.
- Maintains a wary professional rivalry with the [[Grand Cyclical Archive]].
- Keeps copies of major contracts for the [[Guild of Conduits]].
- Hires discreet messengers from [[The Smouldering Cog]] when official courier routes feel too watched.

## Campaign Uses

- A forged pipeline inspection order bears a nearly perfect guild seal.
- An apprentice asks the party to recover a courier page that is still writing replies from its stolen twin.
- A damaged ration ledger reveals that someone altered numbers after certification.
- A sealed contract cannot be opened until the party finds the missing witness named in its margin.
- The guild offers training or access to [[Living Ink]] in exchange for recovering stolen lampblack made from mana-burned vellum.
- [[The Walking Books|Books in the guild library]] are being enchanted into living manuscripts and literally walking out through vents, windows, and courier slots.
- [[The Unburnable Ledger]] has surfaced in a smuggler dispute, and every faction named inside wants it stolen, hidden, or read first.
- [[The Page Thief]] is stealing the same numbered page from unrelated books; together, the missing pages form a ritual text.
- A shortage of [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] sends the party to [[Moonmere Paper Mill]] before the next clear mordanting night.
