# Living Ink Guild Overview

## Purpose

The [[Living Ink Guild]] is Aethergate's licensed scribing guild: part trade fellowship, part archive service, part legal witness, and part magical calligraphy school.

Its mundane scribes keep the city's documents moving. Its Inkbinders handle written magic: warded pages, oath seals, hidden messages, paired correspondence, living manuscripts, and delicate mixed-realm spell notation.

## Seniority

### Guildmistress

The guildmistress speaks for the guild, approves high-risk commissions, negotiates with city offices, and decides when a written matter has become dangerous enough to hire outside help.

Current guildmistress: [[Ysabet Blackletter]].

### Masters of Hand

Senior specialists who oversee one branch of the guild: contracts, archives, magical calligraphy, copying, illumination, supplies, or public notices.

### Inkbinders

Licensed magical scribes trained to make words hold mana. Inkbinders may prepare living manuscripts, contract seals, paired pages, and protected records.

### Journeymen

Working scribes who copy, certify, restore, translate, illuminate, and witness documents. Many never learn magic but are still highly respected.

### Apprentices

Junior workers who grind pigment, sand pages, trim quills, prepare wax, carry notices, sort records, and learn the terrible discipline of writing the same line perfectly two hundred times.

## Quest-Giver NPCs

- [[Bramm Vellumthumb]]: dwarf supply-master; hires escorts and troubleshooters for paper, ink, wax, and binding materials.

- [[Sskara Thin-Quill]]: saurkur legal hand; tracks the Page Thief and understands written authority.
- [[Elarian Moonreed]]: half-elven Moonmere factor; guides the party toward [[Moonmere Paper Mill]] and [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] problems.
