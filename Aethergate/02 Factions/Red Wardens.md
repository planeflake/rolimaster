# Red Wardens

The Red Wardens are the public-facing name of the Blood King's hidden order. To outsiders, the name suggests civic guards, plague wardens, sealed archive officers, or an old order of watchmen.

Within the order, the true oath is called the Crimson Vow.

## Purpose

The Red Wardens preserve the Blood King's influence across weak mana cycles, protect [[The Red Reliquary]] in [[Veyrholm]], harvest useful blood samples, and keep blood magic hidden behind civic discipline.

They are not merely guards. Their blood carries ranked access to the closed spell list [[Bloodline Sovereignty]].

## Blood and Spell Access

Permanent access to Bloodline Sovereignty requires formal induction into the Red Wardens. The rite is called the Veining.

During the Veining:

1. The initiate gives blood freely.
2. Their blood is mixed with blood from a superior rank.
3. The mixture is sealed with a trace of the Blood King's blood or blood descended from his original rite.
4. The initiate drinks, bleeds, and swears the Crimson Vow.
5. Their blood becomes part of the order's living spell archive.

A Red Warden cannot simply teach the list. Without inducted blood, the spells do not fully answer.

## Taking the Red

Non-members can gain temporary access by using Warden blood in a rite called Taking the Red.

Common methods include:

- A drop under the tongue.
- Blood rubbed into a fresh cut.
- Blood mixed into wine and swallowed.
- Blood placed on the eyelid for sight spells.
- Blood burned in a lamp for distant influence or dream magic.

Temporary access usually lasts until sunrise, for one scene, or for one casting per spell. Stolen blood works, but it is unstable and dangerous.

## Red Recognition

Repeated unauthorized use causes Red Recognition.

Early signs:

- The caster dreams fragments of the donor's memories.
- They wake with the donor's habits, phrases, or emotional scars.
- Their blood reacts to Warden commands.

Advanced signs:

- The Blood King can detect the caster during or after spellcasting.
- The caster feels loyalty toward the donor, the order, or the Blood King.
- The donor may dream back.
- The caster's blood-pattern can be marked remotely.

## Ranks

| Rank | Public Role | Secret Role | Blood Grants |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ward-Pledge | Clerks, cleaners, messengers | Watchers and sample handlers | Minor detection, concealment, and wound control |
| Ash Warden | Plague wardens and infirmary guards | Suppress public bloodshed and collect useful blood | Tracking, fear, marking, and silence |
| Red Warden | Civic officers or elite guards | Field agents of the Crimson Vow | Commands over marked victims, blood shields, and lineage reading |
| Seal-Captain | Warden house captains | Vault wardens, interrogators, and blood-lock keepers | Blood locks, ancestral memory, and lesser heart-stopping magic |
| High Marshal | Envoys, judges, military advisors | Regional commanders of hidden influence | Domination through blood samples and royal claims |
| King's Warden | Rare or mythical personal guards | Living extensions of the Blood King | Bloodline curses, loyalty rewriting, and lesser vault access |
| The Blood King | Dead monarch, saint, myth, or tyrant | Master of the Crimson Vow | The complete list and the master seal of the Red Reliquary |

Each rank's blood grants access only up to that rank's spells.

## Campaign Uses

- A Red Warden appears as a quiet public-health officer investigating an accident in Aethergate.
- A vial of Ash Warden blood reaches the black market and begins giving desperate casters donor memories.
- A High Marshal seeks a royal descendant hiding in the city.
- Someone offers the party a way into Veyrholm that requires Taking the Red.
