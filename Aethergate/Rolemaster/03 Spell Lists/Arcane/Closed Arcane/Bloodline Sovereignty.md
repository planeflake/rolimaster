# Bloodline Sovereignty

## Summary

Bloodline Sovereignty is a closed blood magic list carried by the blood of the [[Red Wardens]] and perfected by [[The Blood King]].

The list is not learned from books. A caster must either join the Red Wardens through the Veining or temporarily use Warden blood through Taking the Red.

Temporary use risks donor-memory dreams, Red Recognition, and detection by the Blood King after repeated casting.

## Campaign Availability

Permanent access requires joining the Red Wardens and swearing the Crimson Vow.

Temporary access requires blood from a ranked member of the order. Each rank's blood grants spells only up to that rank's known limit.
