# The Unburnable Ledger

## Overview

The Unburnable Ledger is a black-bound account book that cannot be burned, drowned, pulped, scraped clean, or made to forget what it has recorded.

It is rumored to contain names, payments, forged inspections, hidden shipments, false ration claims, and private bargains between several Aethergate factions.

## Strange Properties

- Fire blackens the cover but leaves the pages readable.
- Water beads on the ink unless the book wants a page seen.
- Torn pages crawl back toward the binding over time.
- False entries slowly fade unless witnessed by a living signer.
- The book grows warmer near people named inside it.

## Current Situation

The ledger has surfaced in a smuggler dispute connected to forged paperwork and stolen scribing supplies.

The [[Living Ink Guild]] wants it secured before anyone tampers with its pages. The [[Mana Authority]] wants it impounded. Criminal buyers want it because proof is worth more than gold when everyone is guilty.

## Campaign Uses

- A page from the ledger is found soaking unharmed in a vat at [[Moonmere Paper Mill]].
- Someone hires the party to steal the ledger, then another patron hires them to return it unread.
- A dead clerk's signature appears beside a new entry.
- The ledger names one of the party as having already been paid for a job they have not accepted yet.
