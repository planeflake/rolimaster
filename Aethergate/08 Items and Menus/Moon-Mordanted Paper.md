# Moon-Mordanted Paper

## Overview

Moon-Mordanted Paper is a pale, faintly lustrous paper made at [[Moonmere Paper Mill]] outside [[Aethergate]].

"Mordanted" means treated with a fixing agent so ink, dye, or magic will take properly. In Moonmere's craft, the mordant helps moonlight, mana, and written intention bind into the paper without bleeding or tearing the sheet.

## Uses

- Warded letters
- Paired correspondence pages
- Contract seals
- Hidden-margin messages
- Living manuscripts
- Mixed-realm spell notation
- Fine copies for the [[Living Ink Guild]]

## Signs of Quality

Good Moon-Mordanted Paper lies flat, takes ink cleanly, and shows a cool sheen when turned under moonlight.

Bad or tainted paper curls at the corners, drinks ink unevenly, repeats nearby words in faint fibers, or tries to fold itself before anyone writes on it.

## Adventure Hooks

- A shipment is ruined unless the party finds fresh moon-salt before the next clear night.
- A sheet prepared for a living book walks away before binding.
- A forged contract is exposed because the paper rejects a false signature.
- A page from the [[Unburnable Ledger]] refuses to sink in a Moonmere pulp vat.
