# Frost-Veined Siphon Needle

## Overview

The Frost-Veined Siphon Needle is a thin illegal siphon spike threaded with blue-white frost lines. It was built to pierce mana conduits, but something went wrong during use at [[Valve Station 17]].

Now any energy channeled through it is translated into cold.

Fire becomes killing frost. Lightning becomes white static ice. Raw mana becomes freezing vapor. Even warmth drawn from the hand can bead the needle with rime.

## Found At

- Location: [[Valve Station 17]]
- Area: Maintenance Grate

## Character Use

A bearer can use the needle as a conversion focus. When they channel an elemental, energetic, or mana-based effect through it, the effect becomes frost or cold instead of its original energy type.

| Original Energy | Converted Effect |
|---|---|
| Fire | Frost burn, freezing flame, sudden ice bloom |
| Lightning | Crackling cold, numbing white arcs |
| Raw mana | Freezing vapor or pressure frost |
| Heat | Local chill, stolen warmth |
| Light | Pale cold-glow, brittle radiance |
| Force or pressure | Impact with frost shock |

## Suggested Ability

**Frost Conversion:** When the bearer casts or channels an energy effect, they may route it through the needle to convert the effect's energy type to frost or cold.

The effect keeps its original scale, range, and general shape unless the GM decides the conversion changes the fiction. A fire bolt becomes a frost bolt. A flame wall becomes a wall of killing cold. A lightning ward becomes a crackling ice ward.

## Instability

Each use risks backlash because the needle was never meant to hold stable mana.

Possible drawbacks:

- The bearer takes minor cold damage or fatigue.
- The needle chills one carried item until it becomes brittle.
- Nearby water, breath, or blood-flecked residue freezes.
- On a bad failure, the effect partly converts the caster's own body heat.
- Repeated use may attract whatever caused the impossible frost at [[Valve Station 17]].

## Rolemaster Handling

When used under stress, call for a Frost Conversion maneuver. Difficulty should usually be Medium to Very Hard, depending on spell level, pressure, and how violently the original energy resists conversion.

Failure does not need to cancel the spell. It may misconvert it, reduce its intensity, or cause cold backlash.
