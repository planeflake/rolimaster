# Tobiah Flume

![[Portraits/Tobiah Flume - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Tobiah Flume is a young apprentice with the best ears in the [[Guild of Conduits]] and the worst nerve. He can name a leak by its sound from two floors up, but he panics when anyone important asks him to repeat it.

[[Bronn Gearwright]] is training him, which mostly means shouting encouragement.

## Appearance

- Lanky, ink-and-grease-stained, perpetually short of breath
- Carries a brass sounding-horn far too good for an apprentice
- Flinches at sudden bells

## What He Knows

- Has heard the same odd "swallowing" sound at three unrelated valves.
- The sound only comes at the turn of the pressure cycle.
- He wrote it down, but no senior engineer has read his notes.

## Qualities

- Genuinely gifted at sound and pattern
- Honest to a fault
- Desperate to be believed

## Flaws

- Freezes under authority
- Assumes he is wrong before anyone tells him so
- Will follow a braver person into real danger

## Hooks

- He begs the party to come hear the sound before his next shift, off the books.
- His notebook is the first written record of the thing draining the lines.
