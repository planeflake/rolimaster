# Granida Ostvik

![[Portraits/Granida Ostvik - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Granida Ostvik keeps the [[Guild of Conduits]] honest with itself. She logs every pressure reading in the Gauge Hall and cross-checks them against the [[Ratio Office]]'s public figures. She is the first person to notice when two sets of numbers stop agreeing.

## Appearance

- Small, precise gnome with ink-stained cuffs
- A bandolier of coloured marking chalks
- Squints, then writes, then squints again

## Role

- Maintains the guild's true pressure ledger
- Spots discrepancies between guild readings and official quotas
- Quietly distrusts anyone who asks her to "round" a number

## Qualities

- Forgets nothing she has written down
- Fair, exact, and quietly brave
- Will defend her ledger to a director's face

## Flaws

- Pedantic to the point of rudeness
- Trusts numbers more than people
- Slow to act until the data is "complete"

## Hooks

- She has found a steady gap between mana measured at the mains and mana the city admits to having.
- She'll hand the party her figures — if they promise not to let the guild bury them.
