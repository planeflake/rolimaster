# Mira Stoppcock

![[Portraits/Mira Stoppcock - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Guild Master Mira Stoppcock runs the [[Guild of Conduits]] the way she runs a pressure main: no slack, no waste, no surprises. She has kept Aethergate's pipes breathing through three shortages and one near-collapse, and she has the political scars to prove it.

She believes the city is built on the guild's labour and forgets it daily.

## Appearance

- Stout, grey-braided dwarf in a soot-grey coat heavy with valve keys
- Reading lenses pushed up on a creased forehead
- Speaks in short, finished sentences

## Role

- Signs off every major repair and shutdown in the city
- Negotiates (and argues) with the [[Mana Authority]] and the [[Ratio Office]]
- Decides which districts the guild can afford to keep at full pressure

## Qualities

- Reads people as fast as she reads gauges
- Will spend her own reputation to protect a crew
- Remembers every favour, in both directions

## Flaws

- Slow to trust outsiders with guild business
- Buries bad news until she has a fix for it
- Treats [[Bronn Gearwright]]'s warnings as eccentricity she can't quite dismiss

## Hooks

- She quietly funds the party to chase a problem the guild can't be seen investigating.
- She knows the official pipe maps are wrong and is deciding whom to tell.

## Useful Dialogue

> "The city prays to the Tower. The city lives because of my pipes."
