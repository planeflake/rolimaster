# Castellan Aurex Vane

![[Portraits/Castellan Aurex Vane - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Director Aurex Vane runs the [[Mana Authority]] like a man holding a city together with seals and signatures. He believes order is the only thing standing between Aethergate and a dark, silent collapse — and he is not entirely wrong.

He would rather call a crisis "ordinary theft" than admit the supply itself is failing.

## Appearance

- Tall, composed, immaculate in Authority grey
- A ring of office heavy with the master seal
- Never raises his voice; never needs to

## Role

- Sets Authority policy on licensing, audits, and confiscation
- Decides which investigations become public and which disappear
- Manages the fragile peace between the Authority, the [[Guild of Conduits]], and the [[Ratio Office]]

## Qualities

- Genuinely devoted to the city's survival
- Patient, strategic, hard to surprise
- Protects his inspectors, even when he disagrees with them

## Flaws

- Suppresses panic by suppressing truth
- Confuses stability with control
- Will bury [[Inspector Elara Voss]]'s findings if they threaten public calm

## Hooks

- He hires the party for a problem he cannot let his own inspectors touch.
- He knows Arcane conversion is coming and is deciding how long to keep it quiet.

## Useful Dialogue

> "Panic spreads faster than any leak. My job is to keep the city from hearing the truth before it can survive it."
