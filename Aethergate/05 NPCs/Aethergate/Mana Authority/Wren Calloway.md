# Wren Calloway

![[Portraits/Wren Calloway - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Wren Calloway stamps licenses at the Meterhouse counter and hears everything. Clerks are invisible to the powerful, and Wren has learned to use that. They know which refiners are late, which inspectors are bought, and which files keep getting "misplaced."

## Appearance

- Young, sharp-eyed half-elf in an ink-cuffed grey tunic
- A nervous habit of straightening already-straight papers
- Friendly smile that gives nothing away

## Role

- Processes licenses, renewals, and fines at the public counter
- Quietly notices the gaps between what's filed and what's true
- A potential informant for anyone who treats them like a person

## Qualities

- Observant and discreet
- Genuinely decent under the caution
- Remembers faces and favours

## Flaws

- Terrified of losing the only secure job they've ever had
- Will hedge and stall before committing to a side
- Owes a quiet debt to the wrong person

## Hooks

- Wren can tell the party which file vanished and roughly when.
- They want out of something and will trade information for protection.
