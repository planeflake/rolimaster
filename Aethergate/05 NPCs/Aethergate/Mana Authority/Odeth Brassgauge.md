# Odeth Brassgauge

![[Portraits/Odeth Brassgauge - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Senior Inspector Odeth Brassgauge weighs canisters for the [[Mana Authority]] and has never once been fooled by a forged seal — until now. Something is moving mana through the city with papers cleaner than the law itself, and it is keeping her awake.

## Appearance

- Round-spectacled gnome with a portable resonance scale always on her hip
- Smells faintly of the ozone from confiscated canisters
- Taps things to hear how full they are

## What She Knows

- A run of canisters passed every test but "felt" wrong on the scale.
- The seals are genuine Authority seals — which should be impossible.
- Either someone has the master seal, or the canisters are emptier than the law can measure.

## Qualities

- Incorruptible and precise
- Trusts her instruments and her hands equally
- Will not sign what she cannot verify

## Flaws

- Obsessive once a number won't sit right
- Blunt with superiors, including [[Castellan Aurex Vane]]
- Hates being told to "approve it and move on"

## Hooks

- She slips the party a flagged canister and asks them to follow where it goes.
- She suspects the leak is inside the Seal Archive itself.
