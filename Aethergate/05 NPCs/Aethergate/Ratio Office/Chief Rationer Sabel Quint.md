# Chief Rationer Sabel Quint

![[Portraits/Chief Rationer Sabel Quint - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Chief Rationer Sabel Quint decides which districts go dark. Every cycle she signs the figures that dim shrines, pause factories, and keep the [[Ninefold Infirmary]] alive. She has made peace with being hated, because someone has to count what the city would rather pray over.

## Appearance

- Severe, grey-suited, hands stained with permanent ledger ink
- Carries a worn abacus she refuses to replace
- Tired eyes that miss nothing

## Role

- Sets the final rationing figures for the [[Ratio Office]]
- Authorizes [[Mana Conversion]] at [[The Convergence Works]]
- Holds the line on minimum pressure for [[The Heartline]]

## Qualities

- Ruthlessly fair
- Carries the moral weight of her decisions without flinching
- Will not be flattered or threatened off a number

## Flaws

- Trusts the ledger over any human plea
- Isolated; few dare befriend her
- Slow to believe the official reserve figures could be lies

## Hooks

- She privately hires the party because the public numbers no longer match the real reserves.
- She suspects a forecast has been forged to force a [[The Day of Open Valves]].

## Useful Dialogue

> "I do not decide who suffers. The arithmetic does. I only sign it."
