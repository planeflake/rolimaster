# Clerk Hollin Marsh

![[Portraits/Clerk Hollin Marsh - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Hollin Marsh logs reserve reports on the Counting Floor of the [[Ratio Office]] and has begun to suspect he is logging lies. The reserve drums say one thing; the numbers handed to him say another. He is too honest to ignore it and too frightened to report it through proper channels.

## Appearance

- Thin, anxious, perpetually inkstained
- A satchel of copied figures he should not have
- Glances at the door more than he should

## What He Knows

- Incoming reserve reports have been "corrected" before reaching the drums.
- The corrections all shrink the same realm's apparent supply.
- He has kept copies of the originals, against every rule.

## Qualities

- Quietly courageous
- Meticulous record-keeper
- Cannot stomach a falsified ledger

## Flaws

- Scared of his own evidence
- No idea whom above him to trust
- Will hesitate at the worst possible moment

## Hooks

- Hollin smuggles the party his copied figures and begs them to find out who altered them.
- He fears the forgery leads back to someone he reports to.
