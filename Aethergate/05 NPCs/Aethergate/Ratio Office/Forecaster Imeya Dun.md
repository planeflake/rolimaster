# Forecaster Imeya Dun

![[Portraits/Forecaster Imeya Dun - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Imeya Dun predicts when mana will rot. Her [[Mana Decay]] forecasts decide when reservoirs are spent, when conversion is scheduled, and whether the city dares hold [[The Day of Open Valves]]. She is brilliant, exhausted, and increasingly certain that someone is publishing forecasts she never made.

## Appearance

- Wiry half-elf surrounded by charts and weighted strings
- Chalk dust on dark sleeves
- Speaks quickly, in probabilities

## Role

- Models decay risk across realms and reservoirs
- Advises the [[Chief Rationer Sabel Quint|Chief Rationer]] on conversion timing
- Signs the forecasts that justify rationing and festivals

## Qualities

- Genuinely gifted at prediction
- Honest about uncertainty
- Notices when her own work has been tampered with

## Flaws

- Overworked to the edge of error
- Defensive about her models
- Reluctant to accuse a colleague without proof

## Hooks

- A forecast bearing her seal predicts a decay event she knows is false.
- She needs the party to find who is forging her name before the city acts on it.
