# Bramm Vellumthumb

![[Portraits/Bramm Vellumthumb - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Bramm Vellumthumb is the dwarf supply-master of the [[Living Ink Guild]]. He controls paper, ink, wax, quills, nibs, binding thread, ledger chains, page sand, mordants, and anything else a scribe claims is urgently necessary.

His beard is tied with brass clips stamped with inventory numbers.

## Seniority

Bramm is a Master of Hand, formally titled Master of Stores. Even Inkbinders must sign his ledgers if they want rare materials.

## Personality

- Gruff but generous with competent workers
- Treats waste as a moral failing
- Can identify paper by smell
- Keeps emergency supplies hidden from everyone, including himself

## Quest Use

Bramm gives practical supply quests tied to [[Moonmere Paper Mill]], [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]], rare inks, wax, brass nibs, and stolen shipments.

## Hooks

- He hires the party to escort a wagon of [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] from Moonmere.
- A crate of brass nibs has been replaced by nibs that write in an unknown hand.
- His inventory ledger lists supplies that were removed tomorrow.
