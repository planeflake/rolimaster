# Pella Underleaf

![[Portraits/Pella Underleaf - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Pella Underleaf is a gnome apprentice archivist assigned to the basement records stack of the [[Living Ink Guild]].

She is the first person to admit that books are walking out of the library because she is the first person small enough, stubborn enough, and embarrassed enough to chase them through the courier slots.

## Seniority

Pella is low-ranking but has useful access. She knows shelf routes, servant stairs, archive habits, and which senior scribes pretend not to nap in the proofing gallery.

## Personality

- Nervous, brilliant, and over-caffeinated
- Apologizes to books before opening them
- Notices small changes others miss
- Carries too many keys for someone officially trusted with none

## Quest Use

Pella is the best first contact for [[The Walking Books]]. She can give the party a funny, urgent, low-status entrance into a much larger problem.

## Hooks

- She begs the party to help catch a runaway atlas before Ysabet learns it escaped.
- A living book whispered Pella's name before leaving.
- She found tiny folded legs made of [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]] in the archive dust.
