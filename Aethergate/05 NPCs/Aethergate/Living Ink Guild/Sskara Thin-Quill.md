# Sskara Thin-Quill

![[Portraits/Sskara Thin-Quill - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Sskara Thin-Quill is a saurkur legal hand of the [[Living Ink Guild]], narrow-snouted, amber-eyed, and famous for reading contracts without blinking.

They specialize in civic forms, witness clauses, oath wording, and the small legal phrases that decide whether a person is protected, ruined, or erased.

## Seniority

Sskara is a journeyman with unusual authority in legal matters. Masters consult them when a document's wording is more dangerous than its magic.

## Personality

- Patient, dry, and unnervingly literal
- Hates sloppy aliases
- Enjoys finding the one word that changes everything
- Trusts signatures less than margins

## Quest Use

Sskara gives investigative quests tied to [[The Page Thief]], forged contracts, missing witnesses, false death notices, and stolen civic authority.

## Hooks

- They ask the party to guard a legal book whose page seven is trying to remove itself.
- A replacement page was copied in Sskara's own hand, but they did not write it.
- They believe the Page Thief is assembling a ritual to alter legal identity.
