# Elarian Moonreed

![[Portraits/Elarian Moonreed - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Elarian Moonreed is the half-elven factor who handles trade between [[Moonmere Paper Mill]] and the [[Living Ink Guild]].

They know when paper is ready, when weather will ruin it, when city buyers are lying, and when the marsh has become too quiet.

## Seniority

Elarian is not guild staff, but Inkwell House treats them with the respect due a vital supplier. Without Moonmere's paper, many Inkbinder commissions fail or become dangerously unstable.

## Personality

- Watchful, graceful, and practical
- Speaks of weather like it is a person
- Distrusts city urgency
- Will risk much for papermakers and apprentices

## Quest Use

Elarian sends the party outside Aethergate for [[Moon-Mordanted Paper]], moon-salt, reed harvests, and trouble at [[Moonmere Paper Mill]].

## Hooks

- They need guards for a moonlit harvest.
- Moonmere's drying frames are full of paper trying to walk north.
- They found a scrap from [[The Unburnable Ledger]] in a pulp vat.
