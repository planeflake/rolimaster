# Ysabet Blackletter

![[Portraits/Ysabet Blackletter - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Ysabet Blackletter is the guildmistress of the [[Living Ink Guild]], a severe human woman with silver-streaked black hair, ink-dark fingers, and the kind of calm that makes apprentices stand straighter before they know why.

She treats writing as civic architecture. A bad line, to her, can collapse a family, a business, a ration order, or a city office.

## Seniority

Ysabet is the final authority inside Inkwell House. She can open restricted archive doors, approve Inkbinder commissions, suspend a scribe's seal, and hire outsiders when guild neutrality must be preserved.

## Personality

- Precise, unsentimental, and fair
- Speaks softly when angry
- Remembers exact wording from old conversations
- Believes mercy must also be documented

## Quest Use

Ysabet gives official work tied to [[The Walking Books]], [[The Unburnable Ledger]], and politically dangerous forgeries.

She prefers to hire the party when the guild cannot investigate openly without warning the culprit.

## Hooks

- She asks the party to recover escaped living books without damaging them.
- She wants [[The Unburnable Ledger]] secured before the [[Mana Authority]] buries it.
- She suspects someone inside the guild is enchanting books to walk out of the archive.
