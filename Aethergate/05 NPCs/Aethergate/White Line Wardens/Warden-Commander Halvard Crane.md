# Warden-Commander Halvard Crane

![[Portraits/Warden-Commander Halvard Crane - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Warden-Commander Halvard Crane leads the [[White Line Wardens]] from [[White Line Barracks]]. He has stood at more failing valves than any Warden alive, and he carries every crew he couldn't save. He protects the sick — and enforces the hard arithmetic that decides who else goes dark.

## Appearance

- Broad, scarred, white-enamel breastplate worn dull from use
- A copper pressure mask hung at his belt
- Speaks plainly, decides fast

## Role

- Commands Heartline escorts and hospital-valve security
- Decides when to seal [[The Underworks]] against siphon crews
- Enforces hospital priority during rationing

## Qualities

- Brave, steady, beloved by his Wardens
- Will spend himself before he spends a crew
- Honest even when a lie would be kinder

## Flaws

- Will hide evidence of converted-Essence side effects to prevent panic
- Torn between mercy and the orders he's sworn to
- Carries guilt that clouds his judgement at the worst moment

## Hooks

- He deputizes the party to guard a Heartline repair crew.
- He is sitting on something about [[Mana Conversion]] that he cannot decide whether to reveal.

## Useful Dialogue

> "We are called the Mercy Guard. Mercy is the hardest thing we ration."
