# Mercy-Warden Ilse Tarn

![[Portraits/Mercy-Warden Ilse Tarn - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Ilse Tarn guards the hospital feed at the [[Ninefold Infirmary]]. She has carried emergency canisters through riots and held a valve open with her own hands while a surgeon worked. To the ward staff she is a saint; to siphon crews she is the last thing they want to see.

## Appearance

- Compact, calm, scorched white armband
- Carries a resonance lantern she trusts more than her eyes
- Steady hands, steadier voice

## What She Knows

- The hospital feed has dipped twice when every gauge read normal.
- A Warden pressure mask went missing from the barracks last week.
- Someone has been watching the canister route to the Infirmary.

## Qualities

- Cool under pressure, literally
- Fiercely protective of patients
- Reliable witness with an eye for detail

## Flaws

- Will break orders to save a ward
- Slow to suspect a fellow Warden
- Pushes herself past exhaustion

## Hooks

- She asks the party to carry a sealed canister to the Infirmary without drawing attention.
- She is convinced the missing mask is about to be used against her route.
