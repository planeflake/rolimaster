# Corporal Dax Vey

![[Portraits/Corporal Dax Vey - Portrait.png]]

## Overview

Corporal Dax Vey cuts illegal siphon clamps off protected lines, usually in the dark, usually while someone is still attached to them. He is the [[White Line Wardens]]' sharpest tool against sabotage cells in [[The Underworks]] — and he is starting to enjoy the work a little too much.

## Appearance

- Lean vulfen with a notched mana-shear polearm
- Pressure mask scratched from tunnel work
- Restless, always listening to the dark

## Role

- Leads siphon-cutting raids beneath the city
- Tracks sabotage crews by their clamp-work and residue
- Trains younger Wardens in close tunnel fighting

## Qualities

- Brave to the point of reckless
- Reads a tunnel like a map
- Loyal to his squad above all

## Flaws

- Quick to violence, slow to ask questions
- Distrusts anyone soft on smugglers
- Bends rules he thinks are too gentle

## Hooks

- He wants the party's help cornering a siphon crew that keeps slipping him.
- His harshness may be hiding a cell's true purpose — or covering for someone.
