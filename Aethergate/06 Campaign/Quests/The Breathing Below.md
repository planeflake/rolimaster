# The Breathing Below

## Overview

The [[Guild of Conduits]] is losing mana from lines that show no leaks. The pattern resembles an illegal [[Mana Absorption Arrays|siphon net]] breathing in slow pulses, somewhere beneath Cogmarket. Officials prefer the simpler story of ordinary theft.

## Hook

[[Bronn Gearwright]] or [[Mira Stoppcock]] hires the party off the books to find the source before the next pressure cycle, when the loss peaks.

## Stages

- Hear the "swallowing" sound with [[Tobiah Flume]] at one of the affected valves.
- Cross-check [[Granida Ostvik]]'s ledger to map where mana vanishes before it reaches the surface lines.
- Follow the readings into the old works beneath Cogmarket and the guild's [[Conduit House Floor Plan|Dead Junction]].
- Find the illegal array — and whoever is feeding it.

## Complications

- The Dead Junction's gauges twitch only at the turn of the cycle, leaving a narrow window.
- The [[Mana Authority]] is treating the case as smuggling and may seize evidence.
- Cutting the array wrong could collapse pressure to a whole district.

## Reward

- Guild favour, old pipe maps, and access to routes no official map shows.
