# A Forged Forecast

## Overview

A [[Mana Decay]] forecast bearing [[Forecaster Imeya Dun]]'s seal predicts a decay event she never modelled. If the city acts on it, it will call an unnecessary [[The Day of Open Valves]] — venting precious reserves and handing someone a great deal of vented, "lost" mana.

## Hook

Imeya Dun finds her name on a forecast she didn't write and asks the party to find the forger before the [[Ratio Office]] acts on it.

## Stages

- Compare the forged forecast against Imeya's real models in the Forecast Room.
- Determine who had access to her seal and her methods.
- Trace who profits from an early Day of Open Valves.

## Complications

- The forger understands forecasting — likely a colleague or former student.
- The [[White Line Wardens]] are already preparing festival posts based on the false date.
- Proving the forgery means contradicting an official civic schedule.

## Reward

- Prevented disaster, Imeya's loyalty, and a thread connecting the forgers behind the supply crisis.
