# The Honest Canister

## Overview

Mana is moving through Aethergate in canisters with papers cleaner than the law itself — genuine [[Mana Authority]] seals on cargo that should never have passed. Either someone has copied the master seal, or the canisters are emptier than the Authority's instruments can measure.

## Hook

[[Odeth Brassgauge]] slips the party a flagged canister and asks them to follow where it goes, off any official docket.

## Stages

- Trace the canister's route from the Weighing Room out into the city.
- Discover the seals are real — which points at the Seal Archive inside [[The Meterhouse Floor Plan|the Meterhouse]].
- Identify who has access to genuine seals and a reason to move hollow mana.

## Complications

- [[Castellan Aurex Vane]] would rather call it ordinary smuggling and close the case.
- [[Wren Calloway]] knows which file vanished — but is afraid to say.
- Exposing the leak means accusing someone inside the Authority.

## Reward

- Authority credentials, a cleared name, or a powerful enemy — depending on how loud the party is.
