# The Numbers That Don't Add

## Overview

The [[Ratio Office]]'s public reserve figures no longer match its private ones. Someone is "correcting" incoming reports before they reach the sealed reserve drums, and every correction shrinks the same realm's apparent supply. If the figures are lies, the city is rationing against a number that isn't real.

## Hook

[[Chief Rationer Sabel Quint]] hires the party quietly — or [[Clerk Hollin Marsh]] smuggles them his copied originals and begs for help.

## Stages

- Compare Hollin's copied originals against the published rationing notices.
- Trace where the corrections enter the chain on the Counting Floor.
- Reach the [[The Tallyhouse Floor Plan|Discrepancy Room]] and the reserve drums to confirm the true figures.
- Name who is altering the numbers, and why.

## Complications

- The forgery may protect a refinery (see [[Quota Ghost]]) or someone in the [[Mana Authority]].
- Releasing the true numbers could start a panic or a riot.
- Hollin will lose his nerve at the worst moment.

## Reward

- The Chief Rationer's protection, and the truth about the city's real reserves.
