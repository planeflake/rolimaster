# The Mask in Cogmarket

## Overview

A [[White Line Wardens|Warden]] pressure mask has surfaced in Cogmarket, taken from [[White Line Barracks]]. Warden gear opens protected routes and fools tired guards — in the wrong hands, it's a key to the hospital line and the [[The Heartline|Heartline]] valves.

## Hook

[[Mercy-Warden Ilse Tarn]] is certain the missing mask is about to be used against her canister route to the [[Ninefold Infirmary]], and asks the party to recover it quietly.

## Stages

- Track the mask through Cogmarket fences and couriers.
- Learn who bought it and what door it's meant to open.
- Intercept the gear before it reaches a protected valve.

## Complications

- The thief may be a current or former Warden.
- The mask is bait to pull Wardens away from the real target.
- Recovering it loudly tips off whoever planned the breach.

## Reward

- A secured hospital route, Ilse's gratitude, and a lead on the sabotage cells.
