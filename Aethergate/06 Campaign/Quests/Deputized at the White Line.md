# Deputized at the White Line

## Overview

The [[White Line Wardens]] are stretched thin, and a [[The Heartline|Heartline]] repair crew has to work an exposed valve that feeds the [[Ninefold Infirmary]]. With sabotage cells active in [[The Underworks]], the crew needs guards the Wardens can't spare.

## Hook

[[Warden-Commander Halvard Crane]] deputizes the party at [[White Line Barracks Floor Plan|the barracks]] and assigns them to protect the repair crew until the valve is sealed.

## Stages

- Take the oath and draw Warden gear at the barracks.
- Escort the crew to the exposed valve and hold the position during the repair.
- Repel or capture the siphon crew that comes for the open line.

## Complications

- The pressure bell rings while every gauge reads normal.
- [[Corporal Dax Vey]] wants prisoners; the crew chief wants them gone.
- The attackers know the route too well — someone talked.

## Reward

- Warden standing, a Heartline access token, and Crane's trust.
