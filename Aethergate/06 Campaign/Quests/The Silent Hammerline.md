# The Silent Hammerline

## Overview

A section of the Hammerline — one of the great pipelines feeding the city's forges — has gone quiet. No burst, no alarm; it simply stopped speaking to the gauges. The [[Guild of Conduits]] needs a crew to walk the line and find out why before the forges that depend on it go cold.

## Hook

[[Mira Stoppcock]] needs the line checked quietly, before the [[Ratio Office]] reroutes pressure and exposes how little reserve the guild really has.

## Stages

- Walk the silent stretch with a guild crew, sounding for the break.
- Discover the line isn't broken — it's been quietly tapped and re-sealed.
- Decide whether to cut the tap (and lose the lead) or follow it.

## Complications

- The tap is fed from the same kind of array as [[The Breathing Below]].
- A guild crew member may be in on it.
- Whoever did the work is a skilled conduit engineer — possibly guild-trained.

## Reward

- Guild standing and a debt owed by Master Stoppcock herself.
