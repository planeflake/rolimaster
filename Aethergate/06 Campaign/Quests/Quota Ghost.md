# Quota Ghost

## Overview

A licensed refinery is reporting mana it does not produce. On paper its quota is healthy; on the floor, the vats are half-cold. Someone is feeding the [[Mana Authority]] a ghost — phantom mana that balances the city's books while the real supply quietly shrinks.

## Hook

[[Inspector Elara Voss]] pulls the party into an audit she cannot run openly, because the falsified numbers protect someone above her.

## Stages

- Audit the refinery against its own deliveries and the [[Ratio Office]]'s reserve figures.
- Find the gap between reported mana and real mana.
- Follow the ghost back to whoever benefits from the city believing it has more than it does.

## Complications

- The forged quota may connect to the altered reserve reports in [[The Numbers That Don't Add]].
- Pulling the thread threatens the Authority's careful public calm.
- The refinery owner has friends in the [[Ratio Office]].

## Reward

- A genuine lead toward the deeper supply crisis, and Voss's trust.
