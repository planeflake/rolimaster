# Airship Passage Quest Board

## Campaign Premise

The party needs to raise enough money to take an airship out of [[Aethergate]].

[[Rusk Brinehook]] can arrange passage, but he wants a deposit before he risks taking unknown passengers through tightened city checks. The party can pay in coin, earn a captain's favor, or collect official travel papers that make the flight less dangerous for him.

Suggested target: 60 gp for full passage, or 30 gp plus one strong favor.

## The Smouldering Cog Jobs

| Quest | Patron | Job | Reward | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boiler Songs Wrong | [[Mara Cogswain]] | Find out why the old boiler sings when no city valve is opening. | 8 gp and free room for a week | The sound comes from an old flight-control pipe beneath the tavern, not the modern boiler. |
| The Captain's Lost Ledger | [[Rusk Brinehook]] | Recover a stolen cargo ledger before harbor clerks ground his ship. | 12 gp or reduced passage | The thief only wanted one page: a passenger list marked with false names. |
| Propeller Stage Protection | [[Lira Copperquill]] | Keep a rough airship crew from ruining a packed performance night. | 5 gp and a rumor from every table | The crew is drunk because someone paid them to start a distraction. |

## White Line Barracks Jobs

| Quest | Patron | Job | Reward | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bell Without Pressure | A White Line sergeant | Investigate why a Heartline pressure bell rang when every gauge stayed calm. | 12 gp and a temporary Warden writ | The bell is correct; the gauges have been spoofed. |

## Simple Progress Track

- 0-15 gp: the party is still scraping for passage and taking risky small jobs.
- 16-30 gp: Rusk will hold space for them, but not leave yet.
- 31-45 gp: Rusk accepts coin plus a favor, cargo work, or official papers.
- 46-60 gp: the party can buy passage cleanly.

## Escalation Clues

Use one clue after every one or two completed jobs:

- Several unrelated gauges report more mana than the lines actually hold.
- Blue wax paper appears in both tavern deliveries and hospital supply crates.
- A stolen Warden mask was modified to filter converted Essence.
- Patients at the Infirmary dream of a door below Cogmarket.
- Rusk's passenger list includes a false name tied to the pipeline thefts.

## Running Notes

These jobs should feel practical and money-driven at first. The party is not solving Aethergate yet; they are trying to buy a way out. The city keeps putting the same pattern in front of them anyway: false readings, stolen supplies, Heartline anxiety, and someone using ordinary crime as camouflage.

If the party wants a faster route, let them work directly for [[Rusk Brinehook]] as guards or hidden cargo hands. That earns passage but also puts the next problem aboard the airship.
